impots.gouv.fr
▼
Étapes
  • Allez sur impots.gouv.fr et connectez-vous.

  • Justificatifs de charges déductibles.

Hello! How can I help you?

Select a procedure below. You will see the official link, the steps to follow, and the documents you need.

🧾
Tax declaration
impots.gouv.fr
▼
Steps
  • Go to impots.gouv.fr and sign in.
  • Click on declare my income.
  • Check the pre-filled information.
  • Add missing income or deductions.
  • Validate and sign your declaration.
Documents
  • Tax number.
  • Pay slips.
  • Proof of deductible expenses.
Declaration is mandatory every year. First time: create your account with your tax number.
Go to impots.gouv.fr
🪪
Residence permit
ANEF
▼
Steps
  • Go to the ANEF portal.
  • Choose your request.
  • Fill out the form.
  • Upload your documents.
  • Track the file online.
Documents
  • Valid passport.
  • Current permit if renewing.
  • Proof of address.
Apply before your current permit expires.
